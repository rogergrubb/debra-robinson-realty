Meet Debra

A rare combination, built over decades

Debra Robinson has spent more than 30 years in the world of antiques and over 21 years as a licensed REALTOR® in the East Bay. That unusual pairing is exactly what makes her different: she understands the value of what's inside a home as deeply as the value of the home itself.

Self-employed for 25+ years with a background in business management and interior design, Debra brings organization, calm, and genuine care to families during what is often a stressful, emotional time.
